## 📑 Description

**Weekly Whispers** is an online journal where it is designed to capture your week - one day at a time. It helps you reflect on your thoughts, moods, and experiences through short daily summaries. Each week serves as a fresh start, clearing the slate for new memories and moments to take shape. At the end of the week, your entries reset to make room for the next chapter. This system encourages mindfulness and the idea of letting go - that not everything has to be kept forever. It’s about living in the moment and appreciating how your thoughts and feelings change over time.**

## 🌐 Web Pages

### 🏠 Home Page
The **Home page** welcomes visitors into Weekly Whispers, a peaceful space designed to collect fleeting thoughts and emotions before they drift away with the new week. Its warm, dreamy aesthetic features flowing visuals - soft yellows, gentle blues, and subtle motion, evoking the feeling of floating through calm water. A clean navigation bar allows easy access to other sections of the site.

### 📅 Archive Page
The **Archive page** is where past weeks find their place. Here, saved entries are stored and displayed chronologically in softly shaded cards that fade as they get older, symbolizing the gentle blur of memory over time.
Users can scroll through previous weeks, read summaries, or open detailed entries if they chose to save them. A search and filter feature allows users to look back at specific moods or weeks that stood out.

### 💬 Contact Page
The **Contact page** provides an easy and personal way for users to connect with the Weekly Whispers team. Links to our socials, inviting visitors to share feedback, report issues, or send thoughts about their journaling experience.

### 📼 Forum Page
The **Forum page** provides communication within the weekly whispers community. A simple place for people to share thoughts and feels, experiences and moments, and so much more.

## 💻 **How JavaScript Will Be Used** 
It powers the weekly reset system, automatically refreshing journal entries every seven days while preserving saved ones in the Archive. It also controls entry animations, letting text and visuals fade in smoothly. In the Forum, Javascript handles the data storing, enabling seamless image transitions that move with a slow, floating rhythm.
For the Journal page, it tracks the day of the week, and prompts users before a reset to ensure they can save their words.
Lastly, on the Contact page, it leads to our team's official socials, enabling the creators to have feedback.
